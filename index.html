重點整理
  • 指引的順序：二代抗組織胺 → 控制不好加到 4 倍 → 仍不好加上 omalizumab（首選）或 dupilumab，抗組織胺繼續吃 → 都不夠才用 ciclosporin1。
  • 健保：生物製劑 CSU 健保不給付、要自費：omalizumab 的給付規定 6.2.6 只限重度持續性氣喘，dupilumab 的 6.2.9、13.17 也不含 CSU2。以健保價計，每 4 週 Xolair 約 28,000、Dupixent 約 31,000、Omlyclo 約 14,000 元；病人實際付的是醫院自費價，通常更高（醫院公開價：Xolair 每 4 週約 31,000–34,000 元）2,7。
  • omalizumab 打了不夠好：指引建議加量或縮短間隔，或改 ciclosporin1。Dupixent 在曾用過 omalizumab 的病人身上（CUPID Study B）沒有達到主要指標6。
  • 拉長間隔：omalizumab 完全控制 3 個月後，逐步拉長打針間隔，直到風疹塊再出現1。改成每 8 週打，每 4 週 Xolair 約 14,000、Omlyclo 約 7,000 元2。

Summary
  • Guideline order: Second-generation antihistamine → if not controlled, up to 4× the dose → if still not controlled, add omalizumab (first choice) or dupilumab and keep the antihistamine → ciclosporin only if these are not enough1.
  • NHI: NHI does not cover biologics for CSU; patients pay themselves. Omalizumab rule 6.2.6 covers only severe persistent asthma; dupilumab rules 6.2.9 and 13.17 do not include CSU2. At NHI prices, per 4 weeks: Xolair about NT$28,000, Dupixent about NT$31,000, Omlyclo about NT$14,000. Patients pay the hospital self-pay price, which is usually higher (hospital lists: Xolair about NT$31,000–34,000 per 4 weeks)2,7.
  • Not enough response to omalizumab: The guideline suggests a higher dose or a shorter interval, or switching to ciclosporin1. Dupixent did not meet its primary endpoint in omalizumab-experienced patients (CUPID Study B)6.
  • Extending the interval: After 3 months of complete control on omalizumab, extend the injection interval step by step until hives return1. Every 8 weeks, Xolair costs about NT$14,000 and Omlyclo about NT$7,000 per 4 weeks2.

重點整理
  • 指引的順序：保濕＋外用類固醇，外用鈣調磷酸酶抑制劑為第二線（見下方外用藥表）→ 中重度控制不好：照光、傳統口服藥、生物製劑或 JAK 抑制劑。AAD 2024 指引對 dupilumab、tralokinumab 與 JAK 抑制劑強烈建議，照光與傳統口服藥條件式建議，全身性類固醇條件式不建議1。
  • 健保（12 歲以上）：病灶持續 6 個月以上；EASI ≥ 16、紅腫面積 ≥ 30%、IGA 3–4；先做完照光 12 週，加上 methotrexate、azathioprine、環孢素至少兩種、各足量 12 週無效（或有副作用、禁忌）；光敏感等醫療因素無法照光者可免照光；需事前審查，第 16 週要達 EASI 502。
  • 健保給付：Dupixent（6 歲以上）、Ebglyss、Rinvoq、Cibinqo（12 歲以上）。Olumiant 有許可證但健保不給付異位性皮膚炎2,3。
  • 暫緩與重新申請：Dupixent 用滿 1 年、Ebglyss、Rinvoq、Cibinqo 用滿 2 年後若 EASI ≤ 16 要暫緩；復發至少 50% 或 EASI ≥ 16 可重新申請，附上次療程前後與這次的照片；停藥超過 3 個月視為新申請2。
  • 拉長間隔：Dupixent 標準劑量滿 1 年、EASI ≤ 7 持續 6 個月以上者，荷蘭登錄研究中 83% 改成每 3–4 週打後仍維持控制10；統合分析顯示效果與每 2 週相當11。健保規定用滿 1 年、EASI ≤ 16 就要暫緩，所以主要適用於暫緩後自費的病人2。

Summary
  • Guideline order: Moisturiser + topical corticosteroid, with topical calcineurin inhibitors second line (see the topical table below) → moderate to severe and not controlled: phototherapy, conventional oral drugs, a biologic or a JAK inhibitor. The AAD 2024 guideline strongly recommends dupilumab, tralokinumab and JAK inhibitors, conditionally recommends phototherapy and conventional oral drugs, and conditionally recommends against systemic corticosteroids1.
  • NHI (age 12+): Lesions for at least 6 months; EASI ≥ 16, affected area ≥ 30%, IGA 3–4. Failed 12 weeks of phototherapy plus at least two of MTX, AZA and ciclosporin, each at full dose for 12 weeks (or side effects or contraindications). Phototherapy can be skipped for medical reasons such as photosensitivity. Pre-approval required; the week 16 target is EASI 502.
  • NHI coverage: Dupixent (age 6+), Ebglyss, Rinvoq, Cibinqo (age 12+). Olumiant is licensed but not NHI-covered for atopic dermatitis2,3.
  • Pausing and reapplying: After 1 year of Dupixent, or 2 years of Ebglyss, Rinvoq or Cibinqo, pause if EASI ≤ 16. Reapply if relapse is at least 50% or EASI ≥ 16, with photos from before and after the last course and from now. A gap of more than 3 months counts as a new application2.
  • Extending the interval: After 1 year of standard-dose Dupixent with EASI ≤ 7 for at least 6 months, 83% stayed controlled on every 3–4 weeks in a Dutch registry10; a meta-analysis found efficacy similar to every 2 weeks11. NHI requires a pause after 1 year if EASI ≤ 16, so this mainly applies to self-paying patients after the pause2.

重點整理
  • 健保的順序：照光、傳統口服藥（methotrexate、ciclosporin、acitretin）→ apremilast 或 deucravacitinib → 生物製劑。2026 年德國版 EuroGuiDerm 指引：第一次用全身性治療時，可考慮直接用有第一線適應症的生物製劑或 deucravacitinib（弱建議）；目標最低 PASI 75，理想 PASI 901。
  • 健保生物製劑：病灶持續 6 個月以上、PASI ≥ 10；先做足量照光，加上 methotrexate、acitretin、ciclosporin、apremilast、deucravacitinib 至少兩種，每種至少 3 個月無效；需事前審查，6 個月一個療程，續用要達 PASI 50（risankizumab 要 PASI 75）；用滿 2 年且 PASI ≤ 10 要暫緩2。
  • 健保口服標靶藥：apremilast、deucravacitinib 只要照光加 methotrexate 或 ciclosporin 其中一種無效即可申請；不能和生物製劑、tofacitinib、ciclosporin 併用2。
  • 療效與省錢：Cochrane 2025 統合分析中，PASI 90 最好的是 bimekizumab、ixekizumab、risankizumab（排第一的 infliximab 在台灣沒有乾癬適應症）3,4。adalimumab 生物相似藥每支便宜 1,500 元（17%），ustekinumab 生物相似藥便宜 15%2。

Example: how Taiwan's NHI decides (biologics)

  1. Criteria: lesions for ≥ 6 months, PASI ≥ 10.
  2. Treatments first: adequate phototherapy plus at least two of MTX, acitretin, ciclosporin, apremilast and deucravacitinib, each for at least 3 months without response.
  3. Pre-approval: copies of records and the phototherapy dose log; latent TB treated for 4 weeks first.
  4. Review: PASI 25 at week 12 for most drugs (week 16 for Stelara, Bimzelx), PASI 75 at week 16 for Skyrizi; renew every 6 months with PASI 50 (PASI 75 for Skyrizi).
  5. Pause: after 2 years, pause if PASI ≤ 10; relapse ≥ 50% allows a new application2.
Full rules
  • Otezla and Sotyktu (8.2.16): phototherapy plus failure of MTX or ciclosporin is enough; renew every 24 weeks; not combined with biologics, tofacitinib or ciclosporin.
  • If control is not ideal (PASI > 1, area > 3%, or lesions on face, hands or nails), another biologic can be swapped in at renewal; both periods count toward the 2 years.
  • Cimzia can be requested in pregnancy or breastfeeding.
  • Excluded: active infection, prosthetic joint, past severe sepsis, cancer or precancer2.

Summary
  • NHI order: Phototherapy, conventional oral drugs (MTX, ciclosporin, acitretin) → apremilast or deucravacitinib → biologic. 2026 German EuroGuiDerm guideline: for the first systemic treatment, consider going straight to a biologic with a first-line indication or deucravacitinib (weak recommendation). Target at least PASI 75, ideally PASI 901.
  • NHI, biologics: Lesions for at least 6 months, PASI ≥ 10. Failed full-dose phototherapy plus at least two of MTX, acitretin, ciclosporin, apremilast and deucravacitinib, each for at least 3 months. Pre-approval required; 6-month courses; continuing needs PASI 50 (PASI 75 for risankizumab). After 2 years, PASI ≤ 10 means a pause2.
  • NHI, oral targeted drugs: Apremilast and deucravacitinib need only failed phototherapy plus one of MTX or ciclosporin. Not combined with biologics, tofacitinib or ciclosporin2.
  • Efficacy and cost: In the Cochrane 2025 meta-analysis, the best for PASI 90 were bimekizumab, ixekizumab and risankizumab (top-ranked infliximab has no psoriasis indication in Taiwan)3,4. Adalimumab biosimilars are NT$1,500 (17%) cheaper per syringe; ustekinumab biosimilars are 15% cheaper2.

慢性自發性蕁麻疹

一句話：指引在抗組織胺加到 4 倍仍不夠時加上 omalizumab；台灣健保不給付任何 CSU 生物製劑，需自費。Omlyclo 健保價為原廠一半，指引也允許控制後拉長間隔；兩者合計，每 4 週費用約為原廠標準用法的四分之一。

1指引怎麼說

2026 年國際蕁麻疹指引1：

  • 建議二代抗組織胺每天固定吃，不是發作才吃；控制不好加到 4 倍（仿單外）。
  • 建議4 倍仍不夠，加上 omalizumab，抗組織胺繼續吃。
  • 傾向建議dupilumab、remibrutinib 也可加上；dupilumab 在合併異位性皮膚炎、氣喘、鼻息肉時特別合理。
  • 反對長期口服類固醇；急性發作可短期使用，最多 10 天。
  • ciclosporin 留給其他治療都無效的嚴重病人（仿單外，副作用較多）。
  • 只做有限的檢查（血球分類、CRP 或 ESR），用 UAS7、UCT 追蹤；每 3–6 個月重新評估是否還需要用藥。

2證據有多強

  • omalizumab 300 mg：第 12 週完全沒症狀 36% 對安慰劑 9%2。
  • dupilumab：第 24 週 30–32% 對 13–17%；但在 omalizumab 用過仍不夠的病人身上（CUPID Study B）沒有達到主要指標3。
  • Omlyclo（omalizumab 生物相似藥）：619 人直接比較，主要指標與原廠等效4。
  • 抗組織胺加量：標準劑量無效的人約 63% 加量後改善，但研究小5。

3台灣健保怎麼規定

抗組織胺有給付。生物製劑用在 CSU 一律不給付：omalizumab 的給付規定 6.2.6 只限重度持續性氣喘，dupilumab 的 6.2.9、13.17 也不含 CSU6。病人要自費，醫院自費價通常高於健保價7。

Chronic spontaneous urticaria

In one line: the guideline adds omalizumab when an antihistamine at up to 4 times the dose is not enough; Taiwan NHI covers no biologic for CSU, so it is self-pay. Omlyclo's NHI price is half of Xolair's, and the guideline allows longer intervals once controlled; together, the cost per 4 weeks is about a quarter of Xolair at the standard dose.

1What the guideline says

International urticaria guideline, 20261:

  • RecommendedA second-generation antihistamine every day, not on demand; if not controlled, up to 4 times the dose (off-label).
  • RecommendedIf 4 times the dose is not enough, add omalizumab and keep the antihistamine.
  • SuggestedDupilumab or remibrutinib can also be added; dupilumab makes most sense with atopic dermatitis, asthma or nasal polyps.
  • AgainstLong-term oral corticosteroids; a short course of up to 10 days for a flare is acceptable.
  • Ciclosporin is kept for severe disease after other treatments fail (off-label, more adverse effects).
  • Limited tests only (blood count with differential, CRP or ESR); track with UAS7 and UCT; review every 3–6 months whether treatment is still needed.

2How strong the evidence is

  • Omalizumab 300 mg: no itch or hives at week 12 in 36% vs 9% with placebo2.
  • Dupilumab: 30–32% vs 13–17% at week 24; it missed its primary endpoint in patients still uncontrolled on omalizumab (CUPID Study B)3.
  • Omlyclo (omalizumab biosimilar): equivalent to Xolair on the primary endpoint in a 619-patient head-to-head trial4.
  • Antihistamine up-dosing: about 63% of standard-dose non-responders improve, but the trials are small5.

3What Taiwan NHI covers

Antihistamines are covered. No biologic is covered for CSU: the omalizumab rule 6.2.6 is for severe persistent asthma only, and the dupilumab rules 6.2.9 and 13.17 do not include CSU6. Patients pay themselves, usually at hospital self-pay prices above the NHI price7.

異位性皮膚炎（中重度）

一句話：美國指引把生物製劑和 JAK 抑制劑列為「強烈建議」，傳統口服藥只是「條件式建議」；台灣健保的順序剛好相反，要先用過照光和兩種傳統口服藥才給付。

1指引怎麼說

2024 年美國皮膚科醫學會（AAD）成人全身性治療指引1：

  • 強烈建議dupilumab、tralokinumab、abrocitinib、baricitinib、upadacitinib。
  • 條件式建議照光、azathioprine、ciclosporin、methotrexate、mycophenolate。
  • 條件式反對全身性類固醇。
  • lebrikizumab（Ebglyss）、nemolizumab 在這份指引發表時還沒核准，沒有被評估。

2證據有多強

  • 單用、第 16 週皮膚改善 75% 以上（EASI-75）：Rinvoq 15 mg 60–70%、30 mg 73–80%；Ebglyss 52–59%；Dupixent 44–51%；安慰劑 10–18%2。各試驗不能直接相減。
  • 直接比較：Rinvoq 30 mg 對 Dupixent，第 16 週 EASI-75 71% 對 61%3；Cibinqo 200 mg 第 2 週止癢比 Dupixent 快（48% 對 26%）4。
  • 代價：JAK 抑制劑有美國黑框警語（嚴重感染、死亡、癌症、心血管事件、血栓）；歐盟建議 65 歲以上、吸菸、有心血管或癌症風險者，沒有其他選擇才用2,5。

3台灣健保怎麼規定

12 歲以上（規定 13.17.1）6：

  • 事前審查；第 16 週要達 EASI 50；6 個月一個療程。
  • Dupixent 用滿 1 年、其他用滿 2 年後若 EASI ≤ 16，要暫緩續用；復發可重新申請。
  • 6–11 歲只給付 Dupixent，條件較寬：外用藥 12 週加一種全身性藥物 12 週無效（規定 13.17.2）。

Atopic dermatitis (moderate to severe)

In one line: the US guideline gives biologics and JAK inhibitors a strong recommendation and conventional oral drugs only a conditional one; Taiwan NHI reverses the order and pays only after phototherapy and two conventional oral drugs.

1What the guideline says

American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) guideline for systemic therapy in adults, 20241:

  • Strong forDupilumab, tralokinumab, abrocitinib, baricitinib, upadacitinib.
  • Conditional forPhototherapy, azathioprine, ciclosporin, methotrexate, mycophenolate.
  • Conditional againstSystemic corticosteroids.
  • Lebrikizumab (Ebglyss) and nemolizumab were not yet approved when the guideline was written and were not assessed.

2How strong the evidence is

  • Monotherapy, at least 75% skin improvement (EASI-75) at week 16: Rinvoq 15 mg 60–70%, 30 mg 73–80%; Ebglyss 52–59%; Dupixent 44–51%; placebo 10–18%2. Trials cannot be compared by simple subtraction.
  • Head to head: Rinvoq 30 mg vs Dupixent, EASI-75 at week 16 71% vs 61%3; Cibinqo 200 mg relieved itch faster than Dupixent at week 2 (48% vs 26%)4.
  • The trade-off: JAK inhibitors carry a US boxed warning (serious infection, death, cancer, cardiovascular events, thrombosis); the EU advises using them at age 65 or older, in smokers, or with cardiovascular or cancer risk only when there is no alternative2,5.

3What Taiwan NHI covers

Age 12 and over (rule 13.17.1)6:

  • Lesions for at least 6 months; EASI ≥ 16, red inflamed area ≥ 30%, IGA 3–4.
  • First, adequate phototherapy for 12 weeks plus at least two of methotrexate, azathioprine and ciclosporin, each at full dose for 12 weeks without response, or stopped for side effects or contraindications; phototherapy can be skipped for photosensitivity or other medical reasons.
  • Pre-approval; EASI 50 needed at week 16; 6 months per course.
  • After 1 year of Dupixent or 2 years of the others, treatment is paused if EASI ≤ 16; a relapse allows a new application.
  • Age 6–11: only Dupixent, on easier terms: 12 weeks of topical therapy plus one systemic drug for 12 weeks without response (rule 13.17.2).

乾癬（中重度斑塊型）

一句話：2026 年歐洲系的指引說第一次用全身性治療就可以考慮生物製劑；台灣健保要先照光加兩種口服藥、各 3 個月無效才給付。療效最好的是 IL-17 和 IL-23 抑制劑；最便宜的生物製劑是 ustekinumab 和 adalimumab 的生物相似藥。

1指引怎麼說

2026 年德國 S3 指引（改編自歐洲 EuroGuiDerm）1：

  • 強烈建議選藥時同時考慮療效、安全性、共病和病人自己的情況。
  • 弱建議第一次用全身性治療時，可考慮有第一線適應症的生物製劑，或 deucravacitinib，特別是嚴重的病人。
  • 治療目標：最低 PASI 75，理想 PASI 90 或 PASI 絕對值 < 2。誘導期結束時評估：多數藥 10–12 週，起效慢的 16–24 週。
  • 建議同樣適用於生物相似藥。

2證據有多強

Cochrane 2025 網絡統合分析：204 個試驗、67,889 人、26 種治療2。

  • PASI 90 最有效：infliximab、xeligekimab、bimekizumab（唯一高確定性）、ixekizumab、risankizumab，彼此效果相近。
  • bimekizumab、ixekizumab、risankizumab 優於 secukinumab、brodalumab、guselkumab。
  • IL-17、IL-23 抑制劑優於 ustekinumab、tildrakizumab、TNF 抑制劑（adalimumab、certolizumab、etanercept）和 deucravacitinib。
  • 所有藥物的嚴重不良事件與安慰劑沒有差異。限制：只涵蓋誘導期（8–24 週）。
  • 在台灣：infliximab 沒有乾癬適應症；xeligekimab 不在台灣3。

3台灣健保怎麼規定

生物製劑（規定 8.2.4.6.1）4：

  • 病灶持續 6 個月以上、PASI ≥ 10。
  • 先做足量照光，加上 methotrexate、acitretin、ciclosporin、apremilast、deucravacitinib 至少兩種，每種至少 3 個月無效；育齡女性可不用 acitretin。
  • 事前審查，6 個月一個療程；續用要達 PASI 50（risankizumab 要 PASI 75）。
  • 用滿 2 年 PASI ≤ 10 要暫緩；控制不夠理想（PASI > 1、面積 > 3% 或臉、手、指甲有病灶）可在續用時平行轉換另一種生物製劑。
  • 懷孕或哺乳時可申請 certolizumab。

apremilast、deucravacitinib（規定 8.2.16）：照光加上 methotrexate 或 ciclosporin 其中一種無效就能申請，門檻比生物製劑低4。

Psoriasis (moderate to severe plaque)

In one line: the 2026 European-based guideline allows a biologic as the first systemic treatment; Taiwan NHI pays only after phototherapy plus two oral drugs have each failed for 3 months. IL-17 and IL-23 inhibitors work best; the cheapest biologics are the ustekinumab and adalimumab biosimilars.

1What the guideline says

German S3 guideline 2026, adapted from the European EuroGuiDerm guideline1:

  • Strongly recommendedWeigh efficacy, safety, comorbidities and the patient's own circumstances when choosing.
  • Weakly suggestedFor the first systemic treatment, consider a biologic with a first-line label, or deucravacitinib, especially in severe disease.
  • Treatment goals: at least PASI 75; ideally PASI 90 or an absolute PASI < 2. Assess at the end of induction: 10–12 weeks for most drugs, 16–24 weeks for slower ones.
  • The recommendations apply equally to biosimilars.

2How strong the evidence is

Cochrane network meta-analysis, 2025: 204 trials, 67,889 people, 26 treatments2.

  • Most effective for PASI 90: infliximab, xeligekimab, bimekizumab (the only high-certainty result), ixekizumab, risankizumab, with similar effects to one another.
  • Bimekizumab, ixekizumab and risankizumab beat secukinumab, brodalumab and guselkumab.
  • IL-17 and IL-23 inhibitors beat ustekinumab, tildrakizumab, TNF inhibitors (adalimumab, certolizumab, etanercept) and deucravacitinib.
  • No drug differed from placebo in serious adverse events. Limitation: induction only (8–24 weeks).
  • In Taiwan: infliximab has no psoriasis licence; xeligekimab is not available3.

3What Taiwan NHI covers

Biologics (rule 8.2.4.6.1)4:

  • Lesions for at least 6 months, PASI ≥ 10.
  • First, adequate phototherapy plus at least two of methotrexate, acitretin, ciclosporin, apremilast and deucravacitinib, each for at least 3 months without response; women of childbearing age may skip acitretin.
  • Pre-approval, 6 months per course; continuation needs PASI 50 (PASI 75 for risankizumab).
  • After 2 years, PASI ≤ 10 means a pause; if control is not ideal (PASI > 1, area > 3%, or lesions on face, hands or nails), another biologic can be swapped in at a renewal.
  • Certolizumab can be requested in pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Apremilast and deucravacitinib (rule 8.2.16): phototherapy plus failure of either methotrexate or ciclosporin is enough, a lower bar than for biologics4.
