# 皮膚病選藥卡 Drug Choice Cards for Skin Disease（試用版 Beta）

開啟網頁 Open the page: https://rogerking928.github.io/drug-choice-cards/

把複雜、更新很快的研究，整理成簡單的表格和圖：慢性自發性蕁麻疹、異位性皮膚炎、乾癬的台灣健保價與醫院自費價範圍、用法、療效、健保給付條件，以及指引怎麼說。
每個數字都附出處（國際指引、樞紐試驗、統合分析、藥品仿單、健保署給付規定、食藥署許可證、醫院公開自費價目）。

我們不做建議，只說明文獻和規定講了什麼。這不是給病人的醫療建議。內容為試用版，可能有錯誤或遺漏，歡迎給意見或來信合作：rogerwang890928@gmail.com

Complex, fast-moving research turned into simple tables and charts for chronic spontaneous urticaria, atopic dermatitis and psoriasis in Taiwan: NHI and hospital self-pay prices, dosing, efficacy, NHI criteria, and what the guidelines say. Every number has a source. We describe the literature and the rules; we do not make recommendations. Not medical advice. This is a beta; feedback and collaboration are welcome.
